Shade Sprint: Colour Tap — Privacy Policy

Android app app.densa.colorrush (formerly Color Rush). Applies to version 1.0.5 and later. Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Shade Sprint: Colour Tap is an offline colour-reflex game. It has no server and does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What we collect and share

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

DataWhere
Best scores, unlocked achievements, Journey stars and Daily progress/streakThe app's own storage on your device only
Settings (sound, music, haptics, reduce effects)The app's own storage on your device only

About Android backup: if backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game save in your own Google device backup or when you move to a new phone. That backup is done and handled by Android under your Google account, not by us; we cannot read it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the save from the device.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share, the app hands a short text about your score to Android's share menu (or copies it to your clipboard). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionPurpose
VIBRATEOptional in-game haptics (can be switched off in Settings). It collects no data.

The app requests no other permission that accesses your data. (Android also adds an internal, app-only permission used by its own components; it grants no access to your data.)

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated.
