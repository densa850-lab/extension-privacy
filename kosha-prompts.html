Kosha Prompts – Privacy Policy

DRAFT · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Kosha Prompts is a browser extension that keeps a library of your prompts and inserts them into the message box on supported AI chat websites (ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini).

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • Your prompts, folders, chat-folder entries and settings are saved in your browser using Chrome's extension storage.
  • If your prompt library is small enough (under Chrome's 8 KB per-item sync limit), a copy is also kept in Chrome's sync storage. If you have turned on Chrome sync, Google syncs it between your own signed-in browsers under Google's privacy policy. Larger libraries stay only on this device.
  • You can export or import your data as a file at any time. Uninstalling the extension deletes its stored data.

Website access

The extension runs only on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com. There it finds the message box to insert a prompt you choose, and reads the titles and links in the site's chat list so you can file chats into your own folders. It does not read your conversations and sends nothing anywhere.

Network

The extension makes no network requests of its own. It loads no remote code. It does not accept messages from other extensions or websites.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your prompts and settings.
  • Access to the four sites above: to insert prompts and show the sidebar there.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
