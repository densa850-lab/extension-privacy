Diya Blast — Privacy Policy

Android app in.densa.diyablast. Applies to version 1.0.2 and later. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Diya Blast is an offline block-puzzle game. It has no server and does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What we collect and share

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no online leaderboard, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

DataWhere
Game progress: unlocked stages, stage stars, best endless score, daily-challenge best score and streakThe app's own storage on your device only
Settings: sound on or off and your chosen themeThe app's own storage on your device only

About Android backup: if backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game save in your own Google device backup or when you move to a new phone. That backup is done and handled by Android under your Google account, not by us; we cannot read it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the save from the device.

Permissions

The app requests no permission that accesses your data. (Android also adds an internal, app-only permission used by its own components; it grants no access to your data.)

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If we ever add online features (such as a leaderboard), we will update this page and its date before that version is released.
