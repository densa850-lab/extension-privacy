Ashlane: Tower Defense — Privacy Policy

Android app com.densa.ashlane. Applies to version 1.0.5 and later. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Ashlane: Tower Defense is an offline tower-defense game. It has no server and does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What we collect and share

Nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

DataWhere
Stage stars, best results per stage and best scoreThe app's own storage on your device only
Settings (graphics quality, and whether you chose to continue on an older Android System WebView)The app's own storage on your device only

About Android backup: if backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game save in your own Google device backup or when you move to a new phone. That backup is done and handled by Android under your Google account, not by us; we cannot read it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the save from the device.

Links to Google Play

If your device's Android System WebView is too old to run the game, the app offers a button that opens its Google Play page so you can update it. Nothing is sent by the app; Google Play handles that page under its own policy.

Permissions

The app requests no permission that accesses your data. (Android also adds an internal, app-only permission used by its own components; it grants no access to your data.)

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date will be updated.
