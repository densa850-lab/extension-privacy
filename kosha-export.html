Kosha Export – AI Chat Exporter: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 (draft)

Kosha Export exports the AI chat open in your current browser tab (works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) to PDF, Markdown or Word files.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or chat content. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, cookies or tracking.

How your chat is handled

Only when you click an export button, the extension reads the conversation in the active tab, converts it inside your browser, and saves the file to your device. The extension makes no network requests and loads no remote code.

Local storage

Your export options (include code blocks, timestamps, title-based filename) are saved in your browser's extension storage. For PDF export, the formatted chat is held briefly in session storage so the print page can open it, then removed. Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

Permissions

  • activeTab, scripting – read the open chat after you click.
  • storage – remember your options.
  • Site access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com only.

Third parties

Kosha Export is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. Product names are used only to describe compatibility.
