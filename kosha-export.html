Kosha Export – AI Chat Exporter: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Kosha Export exports the AI chat open in your current browser tab (works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) to PDF, Markdown or Word files.

What we collect

When you click an export button, the extension reads two kinds of data from the open chat: website content (the page's conversation title and text) and personal communications (the messages between you and the AI service). This data is processed locally in your browser only to create your file, and it is never transmitted to us or anyone else. We do not sell or share any data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, cookies or tracking.

How your chat is handled

Only when you click an export button, the extension reads the conversation in the active tab, converts it inside your browser, and saves the file to your device. The extension makes no network requests and loads no remote code. The chat text is never stored by the extension after the export, except briefly in session storage for PDF export as described below.

Local storage

Your export options (include code blocks, timestamps, title-based filename) are saved in your browser's extension storage. For PDF export, the formatted chat is held briefly in session storage so the print page can open it, then removed. Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

Permissions

  • activeTab, scripting – read the open chat after you click.
  • storage – remember your options.
  • Site access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com only.

Third parties

Kosha Export is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic or Google. Product names are used only to describe compatibility.

Children

Kosha Export is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. It does not send personal information about anyone, of any age, off your device.
