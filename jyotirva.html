JYOTIRVA — Privacy Policy

Android app app.densa.jyotirva. Developer: Den Sa Studio. Effective date: 28 November 2026.

JYOTIRVA (ज्योतिर्वा) is a Hindi-first Vedic astrology app. Kundli charts, Panchang and daily horoscopes are calculated on your device. The app has no account and no sign-in. The free version may show a Google AdMob banner. An optional Pro purchase, made through Google Play, removes ads.

What is stored on your device

DataWhere
Birth details you enter (for example name or relation, date, time and place of birth) and the kundli charts calculated from themThe app's own storage on your device only
Daily readings generated on the deviceThe app's own storage on your device only
Settings: language, Panchang city and chart styleThe app's own storage on your device only
Whether Pro is active (so ads stay off when you are offline)The app's own storage on your device only

This information is not sent to a server, is not used to create an account, and is not placed in ad requests. The app does not add keywords, a content URL or other targeting data to ad requests. Android backup is turned off for this app (allowBackup is false), so this data is not included in a device backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it from the device.

Advertising

The free version may show a banner advertisement at the bottom of some screens. These ads are provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google is a third party, not a service that we operate.

Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which may be used to estimate a general location), app interactions (for example app launches and taps), and diagnostic or performance information, to show ads, measure how ads perform, and prevent fraud. We do not send your birth details, charts or readings to Google.

Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

AdMob and consent (UMP) background: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy.

Banner ads may appear on the home screen, the full daily-horoscope screen and in Settings. They are not shown on the birth-details form. If you are offline, if an ad fails to load, or if you have Pro, the rest of the app continues to work without ads. Ads requested by the app are limited to a PG (parental guidance) content rating.

Advertising ID

To show ads, the app uses the Android advertising ID (also called the AAID or AD_ID) and, where the platform provides it, the advertising-services ID. You can reset the advertising ID (which replaces it with a new one) or delete it (so a new one is not assigned) in your device settings. The exact menu names vary by Android version and phone maker. A typical path is:

  1. Open the device Settings app.
  2. Tap Google → All services → Ads (on some phones: Privacy or Security & privacy → Ads).
  3. Choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

Official help: https://support.google.com/android/answer/12461628?hl=en.

Resetting or deleting the advertising ID does not remove ads from the free version; ads may still be shown, but they are less likely to be based on that identifier.

Consent

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and some other regions, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may ask for your consent before ads are requested. The ads SDK is started, and a banner is requested, only after that flow has finished and the platform says ads may be requested. If the platform says ads may not be requested, no banner is loaded.

You can change that choice later in the app when Google says a change is available: open Settings and tap Ad consent options (Hindi: विज्ञापन सहमति के विकल्प). This button is shown only if you are not on Pro and only if UMP reports that privacy options are required. Tapping it opens the UMP privacy-options form.

Non-personalised ads

If you do not give consent, if you decline the consent form, or if consent cannot be obtained, then either no ads are requested (when the platform says ads may not be requested) or a banner is requested without personalised targeting from this app. In that case the app sends a plain ad request; it does not add keywords, a content URL or its own non-personalised flag. The Google Mobile Ads SDK applies the UMP consent string itself, so a request made after you decline personalised ads is handled by Google as a non-personalised request. Non-personalised ads may still use some technical information (for example IP address or limited device signals) so that an ad can be delivered. Buying Pro removes ads entirely.

Pro purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can optionally buy Pro, which removes ads. Purchases are made only through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your card, bank or UPI details; Google Play collects payment information directly from you under Google Play's terms. The app only learns whether the Pro product has been purchased or restored, and it stores that status on your phone so ads stay off even when you are offline. We do not have a server that keeps a copy of your purchase history.

Content updates (currently disabled)

The app contains an optional, read-only HTTPS fetch of a public JSON file (fresh readings or Panchang). In the current version this address is empty, so the feature is disabled and the app uses only the data bundled with the app. No network request is made for this purpose. If it is switched on later, the request will be HTTPS only and will not include your birth details, charts, advertising IDs or other personal data. We will update this page and its date before any version that turns the fetch on is released.

No account

JYOTIRVA has no account and no sign-in. You do not create a login with us, and the app does not use your Google account to identify you in the app.

Data deletion

To delete the birth details, charts, readings, settings and Pro flag stored by the app, uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings. Because Android backup is turned off for this app, that information is not kept in a device backup.

Advertising data collected by Google is held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings (see Advertising ID above) and use Google's own ads and privacy controls, including Google's privacy policy and Google's ad settings.

Children

JYOTIRVA is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and it is not offered under Google Play's Families policy. Do not use the app if you are under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the changed version of the app is released.

विज्ञापन। निःशुल्क संस्करण में कुछ स्क्रीन के नीचे बैनर विज्ञापन दिख सकता है। ये विज्ञापन Google AdMob से आते हैं। Google एक तीसरा पक्ष है; यह सेवा हम नहीं चलाते। विज्ञापन दिखाने, उनका आकलन करने और धोखाधड़ी रोकने के लिए Google आपकी डिवाइस की विज्ञापन-पहचान, आईपी पते (जिससे सामान्य क्षेत्र का अनुमान हो सकता है), ऐप के उपयोग और कुछ तकनीकी जानकारी ले सकता है। आपके जन्म-विवरण, कुंडली या फल-कथन हम Google को नहीं भेजते। ऐप जो विज्ञापन माँगता है, उनकी सामग्री-रेटिंग PG तक सीमित है। बैनर होम, पूरे दैनिक राशिफल और सेटिंग में आ सकता है; जन्म-विवरण वाले फ़ॉर्म पर नहीं आता।

विज्ञापन-पहचान। विज्ञापन दिखाने के लिए ऐप Android की विज्ञापन-पहचान (AD_ID) और, जहाँ उपलब्ध हो, advertising-services ID का उपयोग कर सकता है। इसे डिवाइस की सेटिंग में रीसेट या मिटाया जा सकता है: आमतौर पर सेटिंग → Google → सभी सेवाएँ → विज्ञापन (कुछ फ़ोन पर गोपनीयता या सुरक्षा और गोपनीयता → विज्ञापन)। आधिकारिक सहायता: https://support.google.com/android/answer/12461628?hl=hi। पहचान रीसेट या मिटाने से निःशुल्क संस्करण के विज्ञापन बंद नहीं होते।

सहमति। यूरोपीय आर्थिक क्षेत्र (EEA), यूनाइटेड किंगडम और कुछ अन्य क्षेत्रों में विज्ञापन से पहले Google का User Messaging Platform (UMP) आपकी सहमति माँग सकता है। विज्ञापन SDK तभी चालू होती है, और बैनर तभी माँगा जाता है, जब यह प्रक्रिया पूरी हो और प्लेटफ़ॉर्म कहे कि विज्ञापन माँगे जा सकते हैं। यदि प्लेटफ़ॉर्म कहे कि विज्ञापन नहीं माँगे जा सकते, तो बैनर नहीं आता। बाद में विकल्प बदलने के लिए, जब Google इसे उपलब्ध बताए: ऐप में सेटिंग खोलें और विज्ञापन सहमति के विकल्प पर टैप करें। यह बटन केवल तब दिखता है जब आप Pro पर नहीं हैं और UMP कहता है कि गोपनीयता-विकल्प आवश्यक हैं। टैप करने पर UMP का फ़ॉर्म खुलता है।

अव्यक्तिगत विज्ञापन। यदि आप सहमति नहीं देते, फ़ॉर्म अस्वीकार करते हैं, या सहमति मिल नहीं पाती, तो या तो विज्ञापन नहीं माँगे जाते, या ऐप बिना अपनी ओर से व्यक्तिगत लक्ष्य-निर्धारण के बैनर माँगता है। ऐप सादा अनुरोध भेजता है; Google Mobile Ads SDK स्वयं UMP की सहमति-स्ट्रिंग लागू करता है। ऐसे विज्ञापनों के लिए कुछ तकनीकी जानकारी (जैसे आईपी पता) अभी भी लग सकती है। Pro लेने पर विज्ञापन पूरी तरह हट जाते हैं।
